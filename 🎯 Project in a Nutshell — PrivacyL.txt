============================================================
1. PROJECT IDENTITY
============================================================

Working concept:

A browser extension + web application that acts as a centralized personal privacy control layer for a user's digital life.

The product helps users:

DISCOVER
→ Understand where their personal data and consents are being used.

UNDERSTAND
→ Explain privacy policies, permissions, data collection and risks in simple language.

CONTROL
→ Revoke consents, manage privacy preferences and initiate privacy actions.

CLEAN UP
→ Identify unnecessary digital accounts, consents and privacy exposure.

PROVE
→ Maintain a trustworthy history/audit trail of privacy-related actions and requests.

The product is related to the DPDP problem statement:

"Privacy That People Can Understand and Organizations Can Prove."

IMPORTANT:

This is NOT a DigiLocker.

This is NOT a government document storage system.

This is NOT primarily for storing Aadhaar, PAN, certificates, government documents, etc.

The core product is:

BROWSER EXTENSION
+
PRIVACY WEB DASHBOARD
+
CENTRALIZED PRIVACY CONTROL
+
PRIVACY ACTION TRACKING
+
PROOF/AUDIT TRAIL

============================================================
2. CORE PRODUCT VISION
============================================================

The central idea:

Users interact with hundreds of websites.

They create accounts.
They provide email addresses.
They provide phone numbers.
They provide names.
They accept marketing consent.
They accept cookies.
They grant permissions.
They subscribe to services.
They create accounts and forget about them.
They later have no idea what data they shared or which permissions remain active.

Our product becomes the user's centralized privacy control layer.

The browser extension works while the user browses.

It detects privacy-relevant events where technically possible.

The web dashboard becomes the permanent control center.

The user can see:

- Websites they have interacted with
- Data categories shared
- Consent given
- Consent status
- Privacy risks
- Privacy actions
- Deletion requests
- Revocation requests
- Activity history
- Recommended privacy cleanup actions

============================================================
5. CRITICAL TECHNICAL REALITY
============================================================

NEVER design the product under the assumption that our platform can automatically control every website.

Websites may not expose APIs.

Account deletion may require authentication.

Consent revocation may require the user to visit the original website.

Therefore support three levels:

LEVEL 1 — DIRECT AUTOMATION

If an official API or supported integration exists:

Our platform can perform the action.

LEVEL 2 — GUIDED ACTION

If direct automation isn't available:

Open the correct website/privacy/account page and guide the user.

LEVEL 3 — REQUEST GENERATION

Generate a structured privacy request for the user.

The dashboard tracks the request.

NEVER falsely claim universal account deletion.
